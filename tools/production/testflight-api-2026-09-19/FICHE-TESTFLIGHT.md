## Notes de validation préparées

Nosfy is a workout tracking app for iPhone (iOS 26 or later), in French and English. Authentication is exclusively Sign in with Apple. The Apple sign-in button creates a new account or restores the existing account; there is no separate username/password login. On first sign-in, select a language, enter a first name and choose a goal. New accounts start without workouts, rewards or cards. Complete a workout to view its stories and earn virtual rewards. Those rewards can be used for card boosters; no purchase is required for this test flow. A completed day in Regularity opens its past workouts. Account deletion is available in Profile > Settings. The backend is online.
